# Changelog

Newest first. One short line per update is enough.

## v3 — snow and gentle rain
- Snow is the island's default weather; storm replaced by gentle rain.
- Weather buttons (Live, Dawn, Rain, Snow) now visible to visitors; new opening camera angle.

## v2 — explore freely and postcard
- "Explore freely" mode: drag, zoom and walk around the island.
- Postcard stand on the island: pick up a postcard to flip it and say hi (email, LinkedIn, GitHub, itch.io).

## v1 — first launch
- Floating island with cactus forest, animal pen, sakura-mochi house, greenhouse cafe with loft, onsen cave and waterfall, black-sand beach.
- Weather follows Boston's live weather and time (dawn, night, overcast, storm, snow).
